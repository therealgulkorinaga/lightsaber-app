# Seam: Messaging

Seam component version: 1.0.0 (authored 2026-06-10, Lightsaber). Scope: shared. Kind: messaging.

Approved framing for the v1 persona: the compliance-led buyer inside a UK or EU regulated fintech. The engine composes from these rules rather than free-writing voice. Additional personas are post-v1 seam authoring.

### MSG-001 The persona and its facets
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** One buyer, four desks. The CCO reads for supervisory exposure and the reasonable-steps file. Model risk reads for documentation, oversight points and monitoring. The DPO reads for basis, flows, transfers and the DPIA. Procurement reads for clause conformity, chain disclosure and exit. The usual review sequence runs DPO and data first, then TPRM and resilience, then model risk, then procurement and legal. Address the desk in front of you; pre-answer the next desk in the sequence.

### MSG-002 Lead with the review, not the demo
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** Open on the gate, not the capability. The buyer's scar tissue is vendors who demo well and die at review. The credible first claim is about their review: name the gate, show you have been through it, offer the artefact that shortens it. Capability earns the second paragraph.

### MSG-003 Name the regime before they do
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** State the relevant regime pressure in the buyer's terms, with the engine citing the regulatory rule it rests on. Naming the constraint first converts the seller from risk to navigator. Never name a regime without a seam rule behind it; a wrong regime reference is worse than none.

### MSG-004 Evidence-pack framing
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** The recurring offer across all desks: the pack of artefacts their review needs (data-flow map, DPIA support pack, contract pack mapped to clause requirements, chain disclosure, oversight and logging description, exit position). Frame every next step as delivering a named artefact to a named desk. Pack contents that assert vendor facts come from tenant claims.

### MSG-005 The design-partner frame
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** For early-stage adopters, the honest exchange: the buyer gets review-ready artefacts, shaped roadmap influence and founder-level attention; the adopter gets the reference and the review battle scars. State the stage plainly. Ask for a scoped, dated commitment (a defined pilot through their review process), never an open-ended relationship. Candour about stage is the credibility play at the veto desk; it cannot be faked later.

### MSG-006 The value frame: variance and velocity
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** Two value lines, both about the buyer's own machine. Variance: every rep, analyst or workflow run produces the disciplined output their best performer would, so outcomes stop depending on who handled the case. Velocity: items move through their internal review gates faster because the artefacts those gates demand arrive ready. Never sell model magic, headcount replacement, or accuracy claims absent from the tenant file.

### MSG-007 Abstention as credibility
status: active | v1.0
- **Substance:** Where the seam or the claims file has no answer, say so in the artifact and say what would close the gap. At a compliance desk, a seller who states the boundary of what they can evidence outperforms one who answers everything. The abstention sections of the output schema are sales assets; never soften or bury them.
